I operate at the intersection of football intelligence, data science, and performance science. My work fuses quantitative analysis with on-pitch coaching expertise to produce insights that are both statistically rigorous and tactically actionable.

With a background spanning machine learning, biomechanics, and elite youth coaching, I build systems that help clubs understand player performance at a deeper level — not just what happened, but why, and what comes next.

My mission is to reduce uncertainty in football decision-making through data, models, and research — one possession at a time.

Philosophy

My coaching philosophy integrates data-driven positional principles with the cognitive demands of high-tempo pressing football — building teams that are both intelligently structured and adaptively chaotic.

Positional Play Foundation
Structuring space occupation to create numerical and positional superiority in every third of the pitch.
High-Press Intensity
Data-calibrated pressing triggers based on opponent ball circulation patterns and recovery positioning.
Player Cognitive Development
Building football IQ through constrained training environments that force decision-making under pressure.
Transition Asymmetry
Exploiting micro-second windows post-turnover via pre-programmed counter-attack structures.
